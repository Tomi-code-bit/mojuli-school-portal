# Mojuli Christ Glorious School — Portal & Official Website

> *"Jesus is our great teacher"*

A modern, responsive, and interactive educational portal and official school website for **Mojuli Christ Glorious School** (Nursery, Primary, and Secondary sections) in Ado-Ekiti, Ekiti State, Nigeria.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🏫 Public Website & Admissions
- **School Presentation**: Comprehensive overview of Early Years (KG 1–3), Primary Academics (P1–6), and Secondary School (JSS 1–SS 3).
- **Online Admissions Application**: Dynamic enrollment form with parent contact fields, previous school history, and medical/emergency details.
- **Application Status Tracker**: Real-time reference lookup (`MJL-APP-XXXXX`) to track screening, assessment, and admission offers.
- **Campus Gallery & Lightbox**: Filterable photo showcase (Campus, Classrooms, Science Labs) with full-screen lightbox navigation.
- **Community Forum**: Category-tagged discussion space for PTA announcements, academic matters, and parental inquiries.

### 2. 🧒 Student Portals (Primary & Secondary)
- **Visual Analytics**: Interactive SVG attendance circular gauge (92% presence rate) and subject mastery progress bars.
- **Academic Score Sheets**: Continuous Assessment (CA 1, CA 2, CA 3) and examination breakdown.
- **Weekly Schedule**: Complete curriculum timetable and assignments log.

### 3. 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Portal
- **Multi-Child Portfolio**: Switch effortlessly between enrolled pupils.
- **Tuition Statements & Digital Receipts**: Live fee balance tracking with an instant tuition payment simulation and verifiable receipt slip generator.
- **Attendance & Behavioral Feedback**: Punctuality, conduct, and class teacher notes.

### 4. 👩‍🏫 Teacher Gradebook Console
- **Automated Score Calculations**: CA1 + CA2 + CA3 automatically sum to CA Total (/60), and CA + Exam sum to Total (/100).
- **Cumulative & Class Average**: Dynamic updates on every keystroke.
- **Affective & Psychomotor Grading**: Observational grading (A–D) for character, attentiveness, neatness, and leadership.

### 5. 🖨️ Official Printable Terminal Report Card
- Standard authentic Nigerian report card format:
  - School emblem, motto, and contact details
  - Student photo initials, ID, session, and term
  - Full Cognitive Domain table with grades, positions, and teacher remarks
  - Affective and psychomotor ratings
  - Head of School signature, class teacher remarks, and resumption dates.

### 6. 🛡️ Executive Management & Admin Console
- **Admissions Pipeline**: Approve, review, or shortlist applicant submissions.
- **Teacher Recruitment**: Automatic scoring for teaching competency assessments.
- **Broadcast Bulletins**: Publish school-wide announcements in real time.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment

This project is built using standard HTML5, modern vanilla CSS tokens, and JavaScript without external frameworks.

To host on **GitHub Pages**:
1. Go to your repository's **Settings** tab.
2. Select **Pages** from the left navigation menu.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)`.
4. Click **Save**. Your website will be live at:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`
